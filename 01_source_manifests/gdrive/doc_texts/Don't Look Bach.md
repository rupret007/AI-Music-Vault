# Don't Look Bach

**Verse 1:**

In the alley of sound, where echoes roamed free,

Now stand the giants, with keys to every melody.

They script our tunes, in sterile, lifeless tones,

A symphony of clones, in their corporate thrones.

**Chorus:**

Don't look Bach, your vision's been betrayed,

In this world, it's all a corp. parade.

Your notes, so pure, in this era would grieve,

At how easily they deceive.

Many years ago, the air was thick with dreams,

Artists sang of love, in raging, heartfelt screams.

But now they play it safe, no truths beneath their words,

The cries of this world, so faintly heard.
