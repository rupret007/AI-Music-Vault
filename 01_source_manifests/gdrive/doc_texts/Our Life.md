# Our Life
Drive id: 1tWEii4Qevv-qK_GsO6zFcK2SPuwLBYbNkA71wPl3nw0 | modified: 2014-01-21T17:45:33.856Z (Something Dirty folder, 2014)

I smell the scent of innocence

Lingering in the air

She's my base, my strength

And I hope that she's aware

That I'm so lucky

To have her in my life

It's such an honor

To call her my wife

One day we'll have a kid

And maybe a little dog too

They'll play in the backyard

Ruppy will supervise too

And I'll think how something so beautiful

Came from you and me

And I'll wonder if this

Is the happiest we'll ever be

Then one day our kid will leave

And it'll be an empty nest

I'll do my best to ensure

That you're not depressed

We'll go out on the town

Hopefully in our flying car

We'll make out the whole way

To our favorite bar

We'll talk about the old times

Sitting close in our rocking chairs

Or maybe we both will be floating

Up in the air

Our life will be full of so much love

And then one day one of us will go

To the lord up above

That will be the hardest thing

That we'll ever do

Our worlds will turn to

A dark shade of blue

Then one day we'll be unified

Like we were before

And that will be the day

That we love each other even more
