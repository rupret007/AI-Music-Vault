## Goal
Hear a full-band arrangement reference for a song that exists only as solo demos — to give Jeff a production sketch he can then re-record with human performances.

## Preserve (non-negotiable)
Melody · lyrics verbatim (from the "11 - Long Long Drive" doc) · key Am · Jeff's conversational phrasing · the quiet devastation of the verses.

## Allowed to change
Instrumentation, dynamics, section transitions, drum feel, the outro treatment (the doc already specifies background-vox call-and-response: "So help me / I need you / Oh how my heart is achin'").

## Style direction (attributes, not artist names)
Mid-tempo (123 BPM) melodic alt-rock in A minor; palm-muted verses with dry intimate vocal; wide-open chorus guitars with the "implode / exposed, woh oh" hook lifted by backing vocals; half-time or stripped-back bridge ("So are we sadistic..."); final chorus with the documented call-and-response background vocals; loose live-band energy, not polished pop.

## Negative instructions
No EDM/synth-pop treatment, no autotune sheen, no tempo change, no lyric changes, no added rap/spoken sections, no female lead vocal.

## Limits
Max 4 useful generations · private only · no publishing · no credit purchase · no voice clone/persona creation.
