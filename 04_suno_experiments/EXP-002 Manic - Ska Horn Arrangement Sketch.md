# Suno Experiment EXP-002 — Manic (ST-0004): ska/punk horn arrangement sketch
**Date created:** 2026-08-19 · **Trigger:** Jeff's first listen to v1.4 — "I hear a horn section like a ska band, it's kinda a ska/punk song."
**Approval:** covered by Jeff's blanket Suno approval (2026-08-18); scope rules applied.

## Source & rights
- Song: Manic — Jeff Story original (lyric lineage 2020→2025, Jeff's docs).
- Source file: **"Manic! (New 05-2024).m4a"** (NEW SONGS 2024 folder) — Jeff's SOLO demo. Clean rights.
- **NOT used:** the album bounce v1.4 or Stalemate Session recordings (band performances — never uploaded).

## Goal
Hear whether Manic wants to be a ska/punk song — 2-3 horn-section arrangement sketches so Jeff can decide with his ears, BEFORE anyone spends a day tracking or programming horns. Output is a decision aid, not a release.

## Preserve
Melody · lyrics verbatim (Manic!! doc, incl. the Riding→Drowning mirror chorus and the "just another perfect manic day!" outro) · tempo/key of the demo · male conversational punk vocal.

## Style direction (attributes)
Ska-punk: upstroke skank guitar on verses, driving punk chorus with the wall of guitars intact, 3-4 piece horn section (trumpet/tenor sax/trombone) playing stabs on the chorus hits and a counter-line riff between vocal phrases; horns lay OUT during verse 1 (build), full section on final chorus; live-band energy, no synth brass.

## Negative
No EDM, no synth horns, no autotune, no tempo change, no reggae half-time verses unless it clearly serves the mania, no lyric changes.

## Limits
Max 4 generations · private · no publish · no credits purchase · no voice clone.

## Decision rule after listening
Adopt horns ONLY if they amplify the mania without burying the mirror-chorus lyric flip. If adopted: album version still ships guitar-only (quick win untouched); horns become a v2 "single mix" project with the band after the album. Either way, log results below.
