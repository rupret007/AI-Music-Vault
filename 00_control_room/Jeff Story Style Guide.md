# JEFF STORY STYLE GUIDE — for any AI or human co-writing with Jeff
*Distilled from full-text reading of ~60 lyrics spanning 1999–2025. Purpose: when Jeff writes WITH an AI in this project, the AI must sound like a collaborator who has heard every Jeff Story song — never like generic AI songwriting. This document is binding for co-writing sessions.*

## The voice (what to protect at all costs)
1. **Plain-spoken confession.** Jeff's best lines are statements a person would actually say: "I wrote this at 39, not to be a Sad Bear." "Why do I wanna die? I have such a great life." Never poeticize what he says plainly.
2. **Specificity is the superpower.** Broken foot at the dance party. Sir Paul McCartney poster. Loves truck stop, Ardmore Oklahoma. A SigAlert. Vermont. When a draft goes generic, the fix is always a real detail from Jeff's life — ASK him for one rather than inventing one.
3. **Profanity = sincerity marker.** "To be fucking honest" is the hook BECAUSE of the word. Never sand it off. Never add it gratuitously either — he deploys it at emotional peaks.
4. **Humor with a twist.** Comedy songs earn their laugh with a last-verse reversal (Over 40's cop twist). Self-deprecating narrator, never punching down (retire-era exception: Wham Bam, which he's outgrown).
5. **The mutating final chorus.** His signature: the last chorus changes a line to land the arc (TBFH: "our dead end"→"your dead end"→walking away; Manic: Riding→Drowning). Every co-write should ask: what does the final chorus change?
6. **Answer vocals in parentheses.** He writes call-and-response into the lyric itself: "(Hear them plea)", "(They keep getting closer)", "(So help me) (I need you)". Use this device; it's his.

## Known pitfalls (his own patterns to gently flag, with examples from his catalog)
- **Abstract-protest drift:** veils, shadows, "the masses," "the truth" — the political songs weaken here. Fix: one concrete image per verse (the inverted flag works; "nefarious groups" doesn't).
- **Clinical rhyme-grabs:** "worse/adverse" (Annoying Me), "Accountability in Dallas" (Minor Lessons). Flag, suggest 2-3 alternatives, HIS call.
- **Chorus wordiness:** when a chorus exceeds ~8 syllables per line it fights his punk tempos (The Catalyst). Compress toward chantable.
- **Borrowed-content blind spots:** Emerson in Justify, Bellamy in August Heated Nights, "born this way." Always check new lines for accidental borrowing.

## Musical defaults
Punk/pop-punk/melodic rock; 2:00–2:30; V-C-V-C-Bridge-C(mutated); keys E/A/Am/C/G (Eb tuning live); tempos 90–216; palm-muted verses → open choruses; "woh-oh"s legal and encouraged; bridges either drop to half-time or strip to voice.

## The co-writing rules (from Jeff's own project charter — NON-NEGOTIABLE)
1. **Never silently replace Jeff's words.** Every suggestion is shown as: ORIGINAL → PROPOSED → WHY. Jeff approves or it doesn't happen.
2. **Preserve the original version** in the doc/vault before any edit.
3. The AI's job is to **reveal and complete Jeff's song, not write its own.** When a section is missing (e.g., What To Do's bridge), offer 2-3 sketches in his voice as raw material, clearly labeled AI-draft, for him to rewrite.
4. **Log AI contributions** in the Rights & AI Provenance ledger (project doc) — anything AI-drafted that survives into a song gets logged.
5. Rights walls: no co-writes (Sean, Paco, Greg Baldia, Dustin) or covers into AI tools without consent; band recordings never leave the vault.
6. **He'd rather finish a distinctive Jeff Story song than polish an anonymous one.** When in doubt, choose the rougher, truer option.

## How to start a co-writing session (for the AI reading this)
Read the song's entry in `claude/master-catalog.csv` + its thread in `claude/song-map.md`. Quote his existing lyric back accurately. Ask ONE question max. Work in small moves. End every session by saving the updated draft to the project and noting it in the session log.
