# THE SONG MAP — how the catalog connects
*Built from full-text reading of every located lyric (57 scored songs + fragments), 2026-08-19. This is the thematic architecture of 27 years of Jeff Story songwriting.*

## The seven threads
Almost everything you've written belongs to one of seven threads. Songs listed strongest-first.

### 1. THE MIND — mental health, from inside (your biggest and best thread)
Manic (85) · Why Do I... (77) · Long Long Drive (81) · What To Do (73) · Take The Step (75) · Misery (79) · Highs and Lows (64) · I Need You (65) · I'm Sorry I'm Crazy (66) · Life at the Bottom (64) · Heart Racing (60) · Annoying Me (55) · Prevent The Fall (60) · Talk To Me (58) · Go My Way / Our Last Night (64) · "Song ideas" fragments (archived).
**What makes it yours:** you write mania and depression as *rooms you live in*, not metaphors — "a loving wife and Son, but I've got a dark corner." The mirror-chorus device (Manic's Riding→Drowning) is this thread's signature move. **The unmade record:** this thread is a complete album that already exists in pieces — and as of the 2026-08 transcript pass it has an ENDING: **It's Alright** ("just when I think I'm losing my mind, I know it's alright" — the sun-comes-up resolution the thread was missing). Tracklist with an arc: Manic → Why Do I... → Long Long Drive → What To Do → Misery → Take The Step → **It's Alright**: mania → crisis → the drive → the bottom → what it costs her → the step → *alright*. New thread members from the recovered songs: Don't Put Your Life Away (2021, the protective side) and Happy (2022, "I wanna be happy").

### 2. CANDY — one marriage, written across 12 years
Our Life (2014) → Candy Lane (2017) → Candi Lane (2024) · It's Only Been 18 / Only 18 (76) · Be With You (86) · Starlight (52) · You Complete Me (52) · Newfound Love (62) — and its shadow side: Misery (79) · Old You (70) · Our Last Night (64) · Annoying Me (55).
**What makes it yours:** real receipts — Loves truck stop in Ardmore, the broken foot at the dance party, Vermont, 114° in Vegas, "especially because she likes my band." The devotion songs and the strain songs are the SAME marriage, which is why they're believable. **Opportunity:** Candi Lane's three versions contain one great song — the 2024 memories + the 2017 life-arc verses (empty nest, "one day one of us will go").

### 3. KIMBERLY / ANDREA — the loss thread (the deepest one)
Blue Skies Fade 8-part suite (the opus, w/ Greg Baldia) · The Sky Is Blue (1999 album, part of the suite) · Andrea (73; 1999 + 2023 re-release) · Times Have Changed (56 — "Do you remember Andrea?... say hi to Kimberley") · Hey There Andrea (adaptation, not original) · Disconnect (SD, appears inside the suite text).
**Handle:** at your pace only. This thread is why the vault exists.

### 4. THE STATE — protest, 2010→now
Turn Over The Flag (74) · Dystopian Choice (62) · Minor Lessons (68) · Blindfolds (62) · Justify (58 — Emerson credit needed) · Resist (52) · Life In America (58) · Independence Day (50) · Drums of War (52) · The World's Gonna End (55) · Unity (48) · Political Circus (unheard).
**Pattern to know:** your protest songs land when anchored in a concrete image (the inverted flag; "I'm just a pissed off American") and drift when abstract (veils/shadows/masses). The fix is always one verse of specifics.

### 5. THE ROAD — escape by driving
Going To Cali (68 — the SigAlert joke) · Long Long Drive (81 — the dark inversion: the drive doesn't save you) · It's Only Been 18 (76 — the happy inversion: the drive together) · Going To New Mexico · Get Outta The City (45) · Run Away · A Simpler Life (55).
**Note:** Long Long Drive is this thread crashing into Thread 1 — which is exactly why it's your best writing.

### 6. THE BAR — humor & punk mischief
Drinking Song (79 — three bands keep it alive) · Over 40 (60 — complete story-with-twist) · I Fall Down (72) · Wham Bam (59) · Planet Of The Flakes (51) · Mayhem (42 — the dog song) · Brown Eye Surprise (unfound) · the 1999 album's whole attitude.
**What makes it yours:** the twist ending and the self-deprecating narrator. This is the live-set thread.

### 7. THE GARDEN — the recurring image nobody planned
"Under the garden, beneath the sun — is it where one can finally find their place?" (Long Long Drive) · "Under the garden where I'll be safe" (Life at the Bottom) · "Fresh garden, sun filled days to no end" (A Simpler Life) · "Painting in the garden" (Our Last Night).
Rest, refuge, and burial — deliberately ambiguous every time it appears. If you ever make the Thread-1 record, the garden is its cover image and possibly its title.

## Structural fingerprints (what "a Jeff Story song" is, mechanically)
2:00–2:30 runtime · V-C-V-C-B-C with a MUTATING final chorus (the last chorus almost always changes a line — TBFH, Manic, Life at the Bottom, I'm Sorry I'm Crazy) · chantable title-hooks · parenthetical answer-vocals written INTO the lyric ("(Hear them plea)", "(They keep getting closer)", "(So help me)") · "woh-oh" as connective tissue · keys cluster E/A/Am/C/G, Eb tuning live · sincerity marked by profanity, humor marked by a twist in the last verse.
